Overview

The Assembly of the Empire is the sole legislative chamber of the Jera Empire. Legislative authority is vested exclusively in the Assembly under Article V of the Constitution. Members may be elected through petition elections or appointed by the Emperor subject to confirmation by the people. No constitutional term limits exist for Assembly members.

Constitutional Authority

According to Article V of the Constitution, the Assembly possesses authority to:

  • Enact laws.
  • Levy taxes.
  • Draft and approve budgets.
  • Conduct censuses.
  • Confirm or reject foreign affairs actions.
  • Exercise all legislative powers granted by the Constitution.

The Assembly may also participate in elections, recalls, constitutional amendments, and confirmation of foreign affairs actions as prescribed throughout the Constitution.
