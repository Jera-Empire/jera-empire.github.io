Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Jera Empire collects, uses, and protects information when you visit or interact with our website.

Last Updated: August 22nd, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jera Empire ("Jera Empire," "we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you may provide while using our website.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect, how we use that information, when we may share it, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Your email address when you contact us or subscribe to communications.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through forms on our website.
  • Information about your interactions with our website.
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and general device information.
  • Browser type, operating system, and other technical information.
  • Website usage information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect for purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining our website.
  • Responding to questions, requests, or inquiries.
  • Providing products, services, or information you request.
  • Improving our website, content, products, and services.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website.
  • Communicating with you about relevant updates or services.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Jera Empire may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website traffic, remember preferences, and provide a better user experience.

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. You can generally control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of portions of our website.

5. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to help us understand how visitors use our website. These services may collect information such as your IP address, browser information, pages visited, referring websites, and approximate geographic information.

We use this information to understand website traffic and improve our website and services.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information with trusted third parties when reasonably necessary to operate our business or website, including:

  • Service providers that help us operate our website or business.
  • Website hosting, analytics, email, or technology providers.

  • Other parties when necessary to protect our rights, property, safety, or users.

Our website may contain links to websites, platforms, or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, provide our services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.
