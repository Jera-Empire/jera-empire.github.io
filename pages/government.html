Government of the Jera Empire

The Government of the Jera Empire is established by the Constitution of the Jera Empire and operates as an Imperial Republic. Governmental authority is divided among the Imperial Crown, the Executive, the Assembly of the Empire, and the Sovereign Judiciary. Each institution exercises powers delegated by the Constitution while operating within a unified sovereign state.

The Emperor

The Emperor serves as the Head of State and constitutional guardian of the Empire. Although the day-to-day administration of government is conducted by the Executive, the Emperor ensures the continuity of the state, oversees constitutional succession, and serves as the Empire's highest constitutional authority.

Constitutional Responsibilities

  • Serves as Head of State.
  • Maintains the continuity and sovereignty of the Empire.
  • Designates an heir through an Imperial Will.
  • Acts as the final judicial authority when required.
  • May appoint Territorial Governors as provided by law.
  • May conduct independent Imperial administrative programs.
  • Assumes executive authority when both the Presidency and Vice Presidency are vacant until lawful successors assume office.

The office of Emperor is non-hereditary. Succession is determined according to the written designation of the reigning Emperor and is subject to constitutional review by the Judiciary.

The President

The President is the Head of Government and Chief Executive of the Jera Empire. Executive authority is vested in the President, who is responsible for administering the government, executing Imperial law, directing foreign affairs, and serving as Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces.

Constitutional Responsibilities

  • Exercises executive authority.
  • Serves as Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces.
  • Signs legislation enacted by the Assembly into law.
  • May veto legislation passed by the Assembly.
  • Administers citizenship applications.
  • Conducts the foreign policy of the Empire.
  • Appoints members of the Sovereign Judiciary.

The President is elected by direct popular vote in accordance with the Constitution.

The Vice President

The Vice President supports the President in the execution of executive responsibilities and serves as the first constitutional successor to the Presidency.

Constitutional Responsibilities

  • Assists the President in executive administration.
  • Succeeds the Presidency upon a vacancy in that office.
  • May perform additional duties assigned by law or by the President.

The Vice President is elected through the Imperial Electoral College, with representation allocated among the territorial districts according to the most recent census approved by the Assembly.

Political Development

Founding Era (2012–2014)

During its founding years, the Empire's political system was heavily influenced by the governmental structure of the United States. Political parties emerged representing a variety of ideologies, including Republican and Marxist movements.

Isolation Period (2014–2026)

Following its early diplomatic activity, the Empire entered a prolonged period focused primarily on internal development, constitutional reform, territorial administration, and government restructuring. During this era, executive authority increasingly centralized while the Assembly continued operating as the legislative institution.

Modern Government

Today the Jera Empire maintains its Imperial Republican form of government while continuing constitutional development and administrative modernization.

Principles of Government
