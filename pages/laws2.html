Laws & Constitution

The founding framework and standing laws of the Jera Empire

Constitution

Article I — The Empire

  1. The Jera Empire is a sovereign, unitary state.
  2. The Empire may not be legally dissolved by any person or institution other than the Emperor, even if the Emperor is the final remaining citizen.
  3. Territorial districts exist solely as administrative divisions of the Empire and possess no inherent sovereignty.

Article II — The Emperor

  1. The Emperor shall be addressed as "Your Sovereign" rather than "Your Majesty."
  2. The monarchy shall be non-hereditary.
  3. Upon death or abdication, the Emperor's designated successor shall inherit the Throne according to the Emperor's written will.
  4. The succession process shall be overseen by an Imperial Executor.
  5. Any succession dispute shall first be adjudicated by the Judiciary.
  6. If no lawful successor can be determined through judicial proceedings, the Empire shall be considered dissolved and any successor state shall be regarded as a new political entity not bound by this Constitution.
  7. Upon the death, resignation, or permanent vacancy of both the President and Vice President, executive authority shall temporarily transfer to the Emperor until lawful successors assume office.
  8. The Emperor may maintain independent administrative functions funded through personal income and any allowance appropriated by the Assembly.

Article III — Citizenship

  1. Citizenship shall be acquired through application.
  2. The Executive shall administer the citizenship process.
  3. Eligibility requirements for citizenship shall be established by law enacted by the Assembly.
  4. All citizens eleven (11) years of age and older shall possess the right to vote and hold public office.

Article IV — Rights and Liberties

  1. Freedom shall be a foundational principle of the Empire.
  2. Citizens shall enjoy freedom of expression, speech, religion, press, and peaceful assembly.
  3. Citizens shall possess the right to keep and bear arms.
  4. Citizens shall be secure against unreasonable searches and seizures.
  5. Additional rights may be recognized by law provided they do not diminish the rights guaranteed herein.

Article V — The Assembly of the Empire

  1. Legislative authority shall be vested in a single chamber known as the Assembly of the Empire.
  2. Members of the Assembly may be elected through petition elections or appointed by the Emperor subject to confirmation by the people.
  3. The Assembly shall possess authority to:
    • Enact laws;
    • Levy taxes;
    • Draft and approve budgets;
    • Conduct censuses;
    • Confirm or reject foreign affairs actions;
    • Exercise all legislative powers granted by this Constitution.
  4. No term limits shall apply to Assembly members.

Article VI — Elections and Recalls

  1. Elections may be called by:
    • Citizen petition;
    • Vote of the Assembly;
    • Order of the Emperor.
  2. A petition signed by at least thirty percent (30%) of eligible voters within the relevant constituency shall be sufficient to trigger a recall election or challenge for a specific office.
  3. No constitutional office shall be subject to term limits unless otherwise established by constitutional amendment.

Article VII — The Executive

Section 1 — The President

  1. Executive authority shall be vested in the President.
  2. The President shall serve as Chief Executive and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces.
  3. Laws passed by the Assembly shall require the President's signature to take effect.
  4. The President may veto legislation passed by the Assembly.

Section 2 — The Vice President

  1. The Vice President shall be elected through an Electoral College representing all territorial districts.
  2. Electoral votes shall be allocated according to a census approved by the Assembly.
  3. Every territory shall retain a minimum proportional stake in the Electoral College, regardless of population.

Section 3 — Succession

  1. The President shall be elected by direct popular vote.
  2. The Vice President shall succeed the President upon vacancy of that office.
  3. If both offices become vacant, authority shall transfer to the Emperor until succession is resolved.

Article VIII — Foreign Affairs

  1. The President and Emperor may initiate or accept diplomatic relations with foreign entities.
  2. The President shall possess final authority in foreign affairs.
  3. The Assembly may confirm or reject foreign agreements, recognitions, and diplomatic actions.

Article IX — Territorial Government

  1. Territorial Governors shall be appointed by the Emperor or elected through petition and confirmation procedures established by law.
  2. Governors shall administer and enforce Imperial law within their territories.
  3. Governors shall possess no independent legislative authority.
  4. Governors may not enact laws, taxes, or regulations contrary to Imperial law.

Article X — Militias

  1. Every territorial district shall possess the right to organize and maintain a district militia.
  2. District militias shall normally operate under the authority of their Governor.
  3. If a Governor is determined to be in secession, district militias shall obey the lawful orders of the President or Emperor.
  4. Secession shall consist of:
    • A declaration of war against the Empire;
    • A judicial finding of contempt or rebellion;
    • A determination by vote of the Assembly.

Article XI — The Judiciary

  1. Judicial authority shall be exercised by the Sovereign Judiciary.
  2. The Judiciary shall consist of up to four members appointed by the President.
  3. The Emperor shall serve as the ultimate judicial authority.
  4. When the Judiciary is evenly divided, the Emperor shall cast the deciding vote.
  5. The Judiciary shall adjudicate constitutional disputes, succession disputes, and other matters established by law.

Article XII — Constitutional Amendments

  1. Amendments may be adopted by:
    • Unanimous approval of the Assembly and signature of the Executive; or
    • A Territorial Constitutional Convention approved by a majority of citizens in a majority of territories.
  2. Governor consent shall normally be required for a territorial convention.
  3. If a Governor refuses consent, citizens may recall the Governor and proceed following the Governor's removal.

Article XIII — Finance

  1. The Assembly shall levy taxes and appropriate public funds.
  2. The Assembly shall establish the annual budget of the Empire.
  3. The Emperor may administer independent programs and initiatives using funds lawfully appropriated to the Imperial Household and personal resources.

Laws

  • The penalty for rebellion is immediate removal.
  • The penalty imposed on theft is to have to pay back in full, or return the item.
  • A politician found guilty of corruption/fraud is removed from office and jailed; this person can, after his release, no longer re-enter politics.
  • Freedom of speech is guaranteed, but violent speech is illegal and punishable.
  • Printing/publishing and spreading violent speech is illegal and punishable.
  • Freedom of religion is guaranteed.
  • Freedom of sexual preference is guaranteed, with same rights for all. (Same-sex marriage is granted.)
  • Women have total equal rights to men.
  • Pension age: 65.
  • All citizens have the right to sue.
  • All citizens have the right to basic education.
  • Minimum wage: 4.50 USD/hour.
  • If one cannot afford to pay his/her debt, the government will provide financial aid at no cost.
  • All citizens have the right to unemployment pay for a maximum of 1 month (Jera jobs only), unless proof of an attempt at job finding is shown.
  • Animals have the right to be protected from inhumane torture and abuse. Any who torture or abuse animals are subject to punishment by law.
  • The regular citizen does have the right to bear arms. Any citizen in possession of illegal firearms will be shot unless he/she surrenders.
  • Pedophilia is a criminal act and is punished by lifelong jail time, without the right of parole.
  • Necrophilia is a crime and punishable by jail time; the corpse is cremated.
  • Child marriages are forbidden. (Anyone under the age of 10 is considered a child.) (Teen marriages must be near same age, by one year older or younger.)
  • Legal voting age / legal driving age / military conscription age / age of consent: 13.
  • Polygamy is a criminal act; the second wife is sent back to the country of origin.
  • Abortion is legal under the following 3 circumstances: rape, threat to the life of the mother, or in case of deformation of the infant.
  • Euthanasia is allowed in the following 1 case: the patient has no possibility of recovery and can't tolerate pain any longer.
