Application for Citizenship

Civil Administration

The Jera Empire welcomes applications from individuals who wish to become citizens of the Empire.

Please complete the following application truthfully and completely. Applications will be reviewed by the appropriate authorities before citizenship is granted.

Citizenship Requirement
Applicants must be at least 13 years of age to be eligible for citizenship in the Jera Empire.
I. Personal Information
Applicants must be at least 13 years old.
II. Citizenship Questions
Please provide your understanding of the Jera Empire historically, culturally, and militarily.
Tell us about skills, knowledge, experience, or talents that you could contribute to the Empire.
III. Citizenship Identification
Why is a mailing address required?
If your citizenship application is approved, the Jera Empire may use the mailing address provided below to send your official citizenship identification card and other physical citizenship materials.
Please provide an address where physical mail can safely be received.
Privacy Notice

Information submitted through this application is intended for the Jera Empire's citizenship administration and will not be published on the Jera Empire website or GitHub repository.

The mailing address provided above is collected for the purpose of delivering citizenship identification and related physical materials.
