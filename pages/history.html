History

Native History

Cahokia, the urban center of the pre-Columbian Mississippian culture, was located near present-day Collinsville, Illinois. That civilization vanished circa 1400–1500 for unknown reasons.

The next major power in the region was the Illiniwek Confederation, a political alliance among several tribes. The Illiniwek gave Illinois its name.

The Illini suffered in the 17th century as Iroquois expansion, caused in part by European expansion in the eastern United States, forced them to compete with several tribes for land. The Illini were eventually replaced in Illinois by the Potawatomi, Miami, Sauk, and other tribes.

European History

French explorers Jacques Marquette and Louis Jolliet explored the Mississippi and Illinois Rivers in 1673. As a result of their exploration, the Illinois Country became part of the French Empire until 1763, when it passed to the British.

The area was ceded to the newly established United States in 1783 and subsequently became part of the Northwest Territory.

Nauvoo and the Latter-day Saint Settlement

In 1839, members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, also known as Mormons, created a settlement they called Nauvoo.

The city, situated on a prominent bend along the Mississippi River, quickly grew to approximately 12,000 inhabitants and for a time rivaled other major cities for the title of largest city in Illinois.

By the early 1840s, the Latter-day Saint Church had built a large stone temple in Nauvoo, which was one of the largest buildings in Illinois at the time. The temple was completed in 1846.

In 1844, the founder of the Latter-day Saint movement, Joseph Smith Jr., was assassinated in nearby Carthage, Illinois. At the time, he was under the protection of the Illinois judicial system and had assurances of his safety from Governor Ford.

In 1846, the Mormons under Brigham Young left Illinois for what would become Utah, which was still then Mexican territory. A small breakaway group remained, but Nauvoo subsequently fell largely into abandonment.

Nauvoo today contains many restored buildings dating from the 1840s, preserving much of the city's historical character.

Modern History

Origins (2012)

The Jera Empire was founded as a hobbyist micronation in November 2012. The Jera Empire has been critiqued for its original name, "Jerapocalpse," since it contains no "Y" in the word "apocalypse."

In response to the criticism, the Emperor gave a brief response: "The Y has no value in the fake word anyway, so what does it matter."

The nation was originally named the Jerapocalpse Empire after the Emperor, but the name was later shortened to simply "The Jera Empire."

Freyan Commonwealth (2012–2013)

The Jera Empire was originally part of the Freyan Commonwealth. It was later expelled following a majority vote due to differences in views between the Jera Empire and other members of the Commonwealth.

First Wars and Alliances (2012–2014)

The Jera Empire engaged in a declared war with the United Soviet States of America across two continuing conflicts, commonly known as the USSA–Jera Empire War and the Second USSA–Jera Empire War.

The Jera Empire experienced many hardships during this period, but continued to exist as a micronation. As of 2026, the USSA no longer exists.

In response to perceived aggression and in an effort to establish a defensive posture, the Jera Empire founded the Federation-Coalition of Nations.

During this period, the Jera Empire also finalized its own constitution in January 2013.

Operation H-War

Throughout its early history, the Jera Empire had an impact on several other micronations. One notable example involved the Doberman Island Republic.

At the time, Emperor Jerapocalpse developed a plan known as "Operation H-War." The operation was named in reference to the discovery that a politician in the Doberman Island Republic was homosexual.

A state within the Doberman Island Republic that adhered to Catholic religious beliefs had reportedly sought genocide. The situation was subsequently stopped, with "Operation H-War" being credited as part of the effort.

As of 2026, the Doberman Island Republic apparently no longer exists. There is no objective date available for its dissolution, although the conflict is understood to have concluded sometime after 2014.

The Jera Isolation Period (2014–2026)

Following the conclusion of its previous conflicts, the Jera Empire largely ceased its international efforts and focused internally on developing the nation.

This included an increased focus on education within the country, particularly within its cyber sphere. During this period, the Empire developed a small industry in computer graphics production and video production, exporting these services to the neighboring United States.

The President and Vice President eventually resigned due to geographical distance from the Empire, feeling that they had little connection to the micronation. Under the Constitution, their resignations granted the Emperor their powers on an interim basis.

The Secession of Blackmill

During this period, the Jera Empire also began experiencing territorial fragmentation. The territorial district of Blackmill and its governor seceded from the Jera Empire and repatriated itself to the United States.

The governor formally renounced his Jera citizenship in 2016. The presumed reason for the repatriation was the Emperor's endorsement of Donald Trump for President of the United States.

The departure of Blackmill eliminated the Empire's small chicken industry.

Political Development (2016–2017)

Between 2016 and 2017, the nation had few significant drives for citizenship, causing its population to dwindle. However, no census was conducted to ensure statistical accuracy.

During this period, new political organizations emerged, including the M.G. Disciples Party and the Losers Party. Both were more cultural than political entities and eventually converted members of the Assembly, removing former party affiliations from governance.

Although members of these fringe parties continued to exist, the Assembly began meeting more cordially. Its intended annual sessions were extended into decade-long terms unless the Emperor called the Assembly into session as provided for by the Constitution.

Transfer to Grove (2018)

In 2018, the administrative seat of the Jera Empire was officially transferred to the district territory of Grove. The District of Jera, however, legally retained its status as the imperial capital.

Following the relocation, the Assembly approved a series of new fiscal budgets that expanded discretionary funding. This period saw a shift in the body's spending priorities, including the legal allocation of public revenues to procure alcohol for internal Assembly consumption.

At the same time, the Empire began reforming its district militia forces, transitioning their inert armaments to live armaments. The Emperor's living expenditures were also allocated toward this security measure.

Rose C. Administration (2019–2020)

The expanded security expenditures initiated in 2018 continued into 2019. That same year, the Assembly called a general election to fill the vacant presidency, resulting in the election of Rose C.

Because no candidates campaigned for the vice presidency, that office remained vacant.

Upon taking office, the Rose C. administration formally codified the Emperor's prior security measures through executive order. Her administration subsequently began restructuring the Empire's governmental and military frameworks, modeling them after the fictional Combine Empire from the video game Half-Life 2.

During this period, the Assembly became entirely composed of members affiliated with the Losers Party. While the legislature approved Rose C.'s subsequent executive orders, it opted not to formally enact them into statutory law.

Centralization of Government (2020)

In August 2020, the executive branch closed its remote office located in California and relocated its operations to the administrative seat in Grove.

This move further centralized the Empire's governmental infrastructure following the initial relocation in 2018, placing all branches of government in one central location.

Pandemic and Presidential Vacancy (2020–2021)

Between 2020 and 2021, the coronavirus pandemic stalled economic activity and relations between the United States and the Jera Empire cooled.

President Rose C. resigned and exiled herself via executive order in order to pursue safety precautions. Her resignation once again left the Presidency vacant.
