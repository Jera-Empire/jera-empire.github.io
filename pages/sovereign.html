Jeremy I

Jeremy I is the founding Emperor of the Jera Empire and serves as the nation's Head of State. His reign has focused on preserving the Empire's history, developing its institutions, expanding diplomatic relations with fellow micronations, and promoting the national identity of the Jera Empire.

As Sovereign, the Emperor exercises constitutional authority, represents the Empire abroad, presides over state ceremonies, and serves as Commander-in-Chief of the Imperial Armed Forces. Through the Imperial Government, he oversees the continued development of national archives, laws, and public administration.

The Office of the Sovereign remains dedicated to ensuring stability, continuity, and the long-term growth of the Empire while preserving its traditions for future generations.

Official Duties

  • Head of State
  • Commander-in-Chief of the Imperial Armed Forces
  • Signs Imperial Decrees
  • Receives Foreign Ambassadors
  • Appoints Ministers and Officials
  • Represents the Jera Empire internationally
