Welcome to the Jera Empire

The Jera Empire is a micronation in North America, completely landlocked by the United States. It is located within several enclaves throughout DuPage County and Will County, Illinois.

Established in 2013, by Emperor Jeremy I. It was founded as a hobbiest micronation. Where it was staffed by similiarly aged people to the Emperor.

This website serves as the official government portal, providing access to state records, laws, historical documents, public announcements, and citizenship information.
